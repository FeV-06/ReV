# What Leaves With Them research for ReV

Use the [research note](../../../docs/research/what-leaves.md) when comparing dependency tracing, backup ownership, or departure questions.

- Trace each proposed risk to the underlying evidence. Distinguish recorded ownership, inferred relationships, and gaps that require confirmation.
- Compare one departure case with the ReV baseline. Record whether each approach finds the affected system, explains why it matters, and produces a concrete next step or question.
- Treat the reference's synthetic graph as a controlled example. Evaluate the cost of deriving equivalent relationships from real Git and Slack records before proposing a graph dependency.
