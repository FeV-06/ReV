# Flamekeeper

[Flamekeeper](https://www.useflamekeeper.com/) is a commercial product reference and a priority for ReV's workflow research. Its public walkthrough shows a handover case, guided knowledge capture, review of gaps and continuity risks, and approval of a final handover. It describes collecting input from the departing employee, their manager, and relevant teammates. These observations are from the public walkthrough.

**ReV application.** Study how the case page shows unfinished work and who can unblock it. For ReV, the natural starting point is an evidence-backed Git and Slack draft. A focused follow-up question can fill a gap when a critical instruction or owner is missing.

**Evaluation.** Open a mock case for an engineer leaving a billing service. Track how quickly a reviewer can find an unresolved risk, route a question to the right person, and approve a handover that gives the successor a concrete first action.
